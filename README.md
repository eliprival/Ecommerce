# Neon Apex F1

A lightweight browser racing experience where you assemble your Formula team and attack neon-lit laps against the clock. Choose a driver, select a futuristic chassis, and push through three laps while keeping your pace inside the glowing circuit.

## Play it locally

1. Open `index.html` in a modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari).
2. Select your driver and car combination from the holographic cards.
3. Hit **Launch Race** and use the keyboard to race:
   - <kbd>W</kbd>/<kbd>Arrow Up</kbd> accelerate
   - <kbd>S</kbd>/<kbd>Arrow Down</kbd> brake
   - <kbd>A</kbd>/<kbd>Arrow Left</kbd> steer left
   - <kbd>D</kbd>/<kbd>Arrow Right</kbd> steer right
   - <kbd>Space</kbd> brake hard

Lap times, best lap, and speed readouts stream through the telemetry HUD as you circulate the track. Complete the three laps to see your session summary and jump back in for another run.
